# Changelog

## v2.0.0 — May 6, 2025 · "The Complete Prop-Firm Suite"

Everything below was built from scratch in the Tier-1 Roadmap sprints.
All features run 100% on your phone — no subscriptions, no cloud servers,
no monthly fees.

---

### 🔔 Smart Notifications (NEW)

Your phone now nudges you at exactly the right moments:

- **Drawdown danger** — pops up the instant your daily or trailing loss
  crosses 70% of the firm's limit. Hard to ignore.
- **Weekly risk budget** — warns at 80% used; hits again at 100% so you
  know to stop for the week.
- **Account locked** — confirms when the app locks you out after
  consecutive losses, with a countdown timer.
- **Loss streak (3+)** — "Pause. Step away. Don't revenge-trade."
- **Daily trade cap reached** — tells you when you've used your allotted
  trades for the day.
- **High-impact news** — NFP, CPI, FOMC alerts about 60 minutes before
  they drop so you can flatten or stay out.
- **Morning mood check-in** — gentle 8:30 AM ping to log how you feel
  before trading (helps spot emotional patterns over time).
- **Weekly backup reminder** — Sunday evening nudge to export an encrypted
  backup (optional, off by default).

All toggles live in **Settings → Notifications**. There's a master
kill-switch plus individual toggles for each category.

---

### 📊 Prop-Firm Drawdown Engine

- Tracks your daily loss, trailing peak-to-trough drawdown, and
  "distance from bust" in real-time.
- Colour-coded dashboard card: green → yellow (40%) → red (70%).
- Works with any firm's rules — FTMO, MyForexFunds, etc.

### 💰 Weekly Risk Budget (R-Units)

- Define how many R you're allowed to lose per week.
- Dashboard shows consumed vs remaining.
- Auto-warns at 80% and auto-stops at 100%.

### 🔒 Auto-Lock After Losses

- Configurable daily trade cap (1 / 2 / 3 / 5 trades).
- If all your trades today are red and you've hit the cap → app
  locks for 24 hours. No override possible.
- Keeps you from blowing the account in a tilt session.

### 📰 High-Impact News Blackout

- Pulls this week's Forex Factory calendar (free, no API key).
- Shows a red banner when a market-moving event is within ±60 min.
- Optionally blocks trade logging within ±15 min of the print.

### 🧠 AI Trading Coach

- Gemini-powered debrief after each trade: what you did well, what
  to fix, and one specific drill for tomorrow.
- Works in local-only mode (rule-based, no internet) if you prefer.
- Costs nothing — uses Gemini's free tier.

### 📅 Weekly AI Digest (Sundays)

- Every Sunday at 6 PM you get a push to tap into your weekly digest.
- Shows: win rate, worst habit, one focus for next week.
- Generated from your actual trade data — not generic advice.

### 😊 Daily Mood Tracking

- Quick emoji-based check-in each morning.
- Tracks correlation between mood and P/L over time.
- Helps you notice "I trade badly when I'm anxious" type patterns.

### 🏆 Personal Edge Engine

- Learns your best setups, times, and pairs from historical data.
- Shows your "edge score" and which patterns actually make you money.
- Highlights setups to avoid (negative expectancy).

### 📈 Tear Sheet (PDF Export)

- One-tap PDF report with equity curve, drawdown chart, session
  breakdown, and key stats.
- Share it with accountability partners or prop-firm auditors.

### ☁️ Encrypted Backups

- AES-256 encrypted export to your phone's storage.
- Share via Google Drive, iCloud, or any app on your phone.
- Import back with one tap — merge or replace.

### 👤 Multi-Account Support

- Track multiple challenges / funded accounts side by side.
- Switch instantly — each account has its own trade history,
  drawdown rules, and risk budget.

### 🛡️ Integrity Log

- Every reset, lock override, balance change, and account switch is
  permanently logged with a timestamp.
- You can never hide from your own behaviour.

### How to Use

1. Open the app.
2. Go to **Settings** to set your prop-firm rules, daily cap, and
   notification preferences.
3. Log trades from the **Trade** tab.
4. Watch the **Dashboard** for real-time risk gauges.
5. Review your **Journal** for patterns.
6. Check the **Edge** tab to see what's actually working.
