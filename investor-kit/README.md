## Quick Stats for Conversations

- **Market**: Online trading platform market $12.57B (2026) → $18.18B (2031), 7.66% CAGR
- **Segment**: Retail investors = 69.59% of trades, growing 8.43% CAGR
- **Problem**: 73% of retail traders lose money (ESMA broker disclosures)
- **Solution**: First pre-trade enforcement app (blocks bad trades before entry)
- **Model**: One-time lifetime deal (launch) → freemium + subscription (growth)
- **Ask**: $50K–$150K pre-seed
- **Use of funds**: App store launch, first 1,000 users, iOS/Android optimization
