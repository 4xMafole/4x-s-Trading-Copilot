## Unit Economics

### Per-User Economics (Base Case, at scale)

| Metric | Value | Calculation |
|--------|-------|-------------|
| CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost) | $3-5 | Primarily organic; paid supplement |
| ARPU (Monthly, all users) | $0.67 | MRR / MAU (includes free users) |
| ARPU (Monthly, paid only) | $11.25 | Weighted avg of $9.99 and $19.99 plans |
| LTV (Lifetime Value, paid) | $90 | $11.25 × 8 months avg retention |
| LTV:CAC ratio | 18:1 | $90 / $5 |
| Payback period | < 1 month | $11.25 first month vs. $5 CAC |
| Gross margin | 85%+ | Low infrastructure costs (Supabase + Gemini free tier) |

### Why Margins Are High
- **AI costs**: Gemini free tier (15 req/min) covers most usage. At scale, $0.001-0.01 per query.
- **Infrastructure**: Supabase Pro ($25/mo) scales to thousands of users
- **No broker integration costs**: We don't connect to brokers (no data fees)
- **Local storage**: Most data stays on-device (minimal cloud costs)
- **No human support at scale**: AI handles coaching; community handles support

## Revenue Sensitivity Analysis

### What Moves Revenue Most?

| Variable | +1% Change | Revenue Impact (Year 1) |
|----------|-----------|------------------------|
| Monthly user growth | +1% (25→26%) | +$2,100 (+11%) |
| Free-to-paid conversion | +1% (7→8%) | +$2,800 (+15%) |
| Monthly churn reduction | -1% (6→5%) | +$1,400 (+7%) |
| Price increase ($9.99→$11.99) | +$2/mo | +$4,500 (+24%) |

**Insight**: Price sensitivity has the highest impact. If retention proves strong, consider $12.99/mo (still below Edgewonk's $12.31/mo equivalent).

## Key Financial Risks

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|--------|-----------|
| Lower-than-expected conversion (< 3%) | Revenue 50% below projections | Focus on activation metrics; improve upgrade triggers |
| High churn (> 10%/month) | LTV drops to $50 | Invest in retention features; community; streaks |
| Gemini API price increase | Margin compression | Fallback to local-only mode; charge for AI separately |
| App Store fee changes | -30% on all in-app revenue | Web-based payment option (bypass store for subscriptions) |
| Slower growth (< 10% MoM) | Longer path to seed-ready | Double down on organic content; consider micro-paid acquisition |
