## Voice & Tone Guidelines

### Brand Voice: "No-BS Disciplinarian Coach"

| Do | Don't |
|----|-------|
| "Your stacking win rate is 8%. Stop." | "Consider reducing your stacking frequency" |
| "You lost 23 straight trades at 09:00. That's your dead zone." | "Morning sessions may not be optimal" |
| "Gates blocked your entry. Good. You were about to revenge trade." | "The system suggests waiting before your next trade" |
| "572 trades don't lie. Here's your edge." | "Based on our analysis, you might consider..." |

### Tone Spectrum

- **App notifications**: Direct, short, urgent ("🔴 3 red trades. Locked for 24h.")
- **Marketing content**: Provocative + educational ("73% of you are losing because of THIS, not your strategy")
- **Social media**: Raw, relatable, data-backed ("Traded EU at 09:00 EAT for 3 months. 23 consecutive losses. Here's the spreadsheet.")
- **Investor communications**: Professional, metrics-driven, vision-clear

### Language Rules

- Never say "might" or "consider" — say "do" or "stop"
- Never use trading jargon without context for non-traders
- Always include a specific number/stat when making a claim
- Use "you" not "users" or "traders" — personal, direct
- Short sentences. No fluff. Every word earns its place.

## Visual Identity Direction

### Color Palette
- **Primary**: Deep navy (#0F172A) — authority, trust, seriousness
- **Accent**: Electric green (#10B981) — profit, gates passing, go-signals
- **Warning**: Amber (#F59E0B) → Red (#EF4444) — drawdown spectrum
- **Background**: Slate (#1E293B) — dark mode native (traders use dark themes)

### Typography
- **Headlines**: Bold, condensed sans-serif (Impact/authority feel)
- **Body**: Clean, legible sans-serif (Inter, SF Pro)
- **Data/Numbers**: Monospace (JetBrains Mono, SF Mono) — precision feel

### Imagery Style
- Real trading data screenshots (with permission)
- Before/after equity curves
- Gate system blocking entries (product shots)
- Dark, moody, professional — NOT gamified or neon-bro
- No stock photos of people in suits pointing at screens
