# LocoTrader — Go-To-Market Strategy

> Phase-based launch plan for a solo founder with zero current traction, targeting global English-speaking markets with Africa-first angle.

---

## Executive Summary

**Launch model**: One-time lifetime deal (limited spots) → free app with premium features funded by initial sales
**Timeline**: 3-6 months to app store launch
**Primary channel**: Organic social (Twitter/X trading community) + community infiltration
**Budget assumption**: $0-$500 (bootstrapped pre-seed)

---

### Waitlist Urgency Framework

```
"LocoTrader Lifetime Deal: $49 one-time
• Only [X] spots available
• When they're gone → price disappears forever
• Includes all future features for life

Join the waitlist. Get notified before public launch."
```

### Beta Tester Selection Criteria
1. Active prop firm trader (can validate drawdown features)
2. Has blown at least 1 account (understands the pain)
3. Active on social media (will share experience)
4. Mix of geographies (Africa, US/EU, SEA)

## Phase 3: Post-Launch Growth (Months 5-6+)

### Objective: Sustainable growth through word-of-mouth and content

### Growth Loops

```
User downloads → Gates block bad trade → User tells friend → Friend downloads
     ↑                                                              |
     └──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Organic Growth Engines

1. **"Gates Blocked" Shareable Moment**
   - When a gate blocks a trade, prompt: "Share this save on Twitter?"
   - Auto-generates: "LocoTrader just stopped me from [revenge trading / trading my dead zone / exceeding daily cap]. The system works. 🚫📊"
   - This is organic, authentic content from real users

2. **Weekly AI Digest Sharing**
   - Sunday digest includes a "Share your week" button
   - One-tap share: "My LocoTrader week: X/X trades won, 0 violations, edge improving. Week 4 streak."

3. **PDF Tear Sheet as Marketing**
   - When users export their PDF tear sheet → LocoTrader branding visible
   - Prop firm auditors, accountability partners see the brand

4. **Referral Program** (post-launch)
   - Refer a friend → both get 1 month of premium features unlocked
   - Or: refer 3 friends → lifetime premium access

### Paid Acquisition (Only if Phase 2 proves product-market fit)

## Geographic Rollout Priority

| Phase | Market | Why |
|-------|--------|-----|
| 1 (Launch) | English-speaking Africa (Nigeria, Kenya, Tanzania, SA) | Home market, growing trading community, mobile-first, underserved |
| 1 (Launch) | US/UK prop firm traders | Largest prop firm market, high willingness to pay |
| 2 (Month 6+) | Southeast Asia (Malaysia, Philippines, Indonesia) | Massive retail trader growth, mobile-first, English-proficient |
| 3 (Month 9+) | South America (Brazil, Colombia) | Growing prop firm market, localization needed |

### Africa-First Advantages
- **Mobile-first market**: 80%+ internet access is via smartphone (matches our mobile-first product)
- **Underserved**: No discipline tools built FOR African traders
- **Growing community**: Forex trading is one of the fastest-growing side hustles in East/West Africa
- **Word-of-mouth culture**: Strong WhatsApp/Telegram group culture for sharing tools
- **Low competition**: TradeZella, TraderSync barely market to Africa
- **Founder authenticity**: "Built in Dar es Salaam by a real trader" = powerful narrative

## Risk Mitigation

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|-----------|
| App store rejection | Submit 4-6 weeks before planned launch; have backup plans for compliance |
| Zero traction on launch day | Pre-built audience of 500+ followers + 200+ waitlist = guaranteed initial users |
| Negative reviews from bugs | Beta test with 20 users for 2-4 weeks before public launch |
| Competitors copy gate feature | Speed to market + brand loyalty + continuous AI improvements |
| Revenue too low from one-time deals | Plan transition to premium features / subscription after lifetime slots filled |
