## One-Time Deal Strategy

### Why This Works for Launch

1. **Removes friction**: No recurring commitment fear
2. **Creates urgency**: "Gone forever" = scarcity trigger
3. **Funds development**: Immediate cash injection without VC
4. **Builds initial user base**: Low price = more users = more data = better AI = better product
5. **Generates testimonials**: 100 paying users at launch = social proof engine

### Risks of One-Time Only

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|--------|-----------|
| Revenue caps out | Can't fund ongoing development | Plan post-deal monetization |
| No MRR for investors | Makes fundraising harder | Show growth metrics instead |
| Users expect everything free forever | Resistance to future pricing | Set clear scope in purchase |
| Undervalued product perception | "If it's $49 it can't be good" | Position as "founding member" exclusive |

### Why These Prices

| Metric | Calculation |
|--------|------------|
| **Floor** | Must be below cheapest competitor ($12/mo Edgewonk) |
| **Ceiling** | Cannot exceed $20/mo (we position on accessibility) |
| **Sweet spot** | $9.99/mo — psychological threshold, < coffee/day positioning |
| **Annual discount** | 35% off monthly (incentivize commitment) |
| **LTV at $9.99/mo** | If avg user stays 8 months → $80 LTV |
| **LTV at $19.99/mo** | If avg user stays 8 months → $160 LTV |

### Pricing Psychology

- **$49 lifetime < 2 months of cheapest competitor** — makes lifetime feel like steal
- **$9.99/mo = "less than 1 losing trade"** — frame against cost of NOT having discipline
- **Free tier must be genuinely useful** — gates are the hook, Edge Map is the upgrade trigger
- **Annual plan saves > 1 month** — clear incentive
